      About & Methodology

      Bantay Proyekto is a proposal. This prototype demonstrates the intended workflow with fictional data.

      The system flags; people verify. A Ghost Score is not a finding of fraud.

      How it would work

      1. 1 · MatchMatch public project records (DPWH, PhilGEPS, Sumbong sa Pangulo) to geographic coordinates.
      2. 2 · LookCompare satellite imagery from before construction with imagery after the reported completion date.
      3. 3 · ScoreGenerate an AI-assisted Ghost Score (0–100) from visible change and data quality. "Completed" with no visible change scores high.
      4. 4 · VerifyHuman inspectors investigate the top-ranked sites and document their findings. Only verified results are reported as confirmed.

      Proposed data sources

      Intended integrations. None are connected in this prototype.

      • DPWH project recordsNot connected

      • Sentinel-1 radar imagery (sees through cloud)Not connected

      Limitations

      • Cloud cover and missing observations. Rainy-season cloud can hide sites; combine many passes and add Sentinel-1 radar.
      • Small structures. Walls narrower than ~10 m are hard to see in Sentinel-2; flagged sites would get sharper 5 m (PhilSA MULA) or commercial images.
      • Incorrect or missing coordinates. A wrong pin can look like "nothing built"; a missing or wrong location is treated as a warning sign, not a conclusion.
      • Ambiguous change. Construction can be hard to separate from nearby land clearing, farming, or flooding.
      • False positives and false negatives. The model will be wrong sometimes, in both directions.
      • Human review required. Every flag needs physical inspection before any conclusion is drawn.

      Who it is for

      • Auditors (COA, Ombudsman, DPWH) — a ranked short list of sites to visit first.
      • Engineers and honest contractors — visible proof that work was done.
      • Citizens and journalists — a public map of where the money went.

      Proposed rollout

      Bulacan pilot→Central Luzon→Nationwide

      Scope: flood-control projects funded 2022–2025.

      • 416 ghost projects validated by the ICI out of ~8,000 inspected — Inquirer, Jan 30, 2026.
      • ₱42.3B–₱118.5B estimated economic loss, 2023–2025 — Dept. of Finance, Sept 2025.
      • Bulacan received nearly 45% of Central Luzon flood funds — press summaries.
